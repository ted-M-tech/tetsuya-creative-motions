# Decisions, not a prompt dump

| Friction | Change | Transferable lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Long explanations hid the mechanism | Three distinct illustrations arranged in reading order | Show the causal sequence before detail |
| Generic generated people felt inconsistent | One unDraw family; actual news photographs | Give each image type a job |
| Hero was crowded | Benefit headline, one CTA, generous viewport space | Remove repeated supporting copy |
| News looked like a generic catalog | Interests → tailored articles → English practice | Show why the content belongs to this person |
| Research became a wall of text | One large 1.3× figure with comparable gain bars | Put the measured quantity next to the number |
| Mobile comparison hid competitors | Compact symbols and responsive layout | Verify the decision on the smallest screen |
| Preview still showed old branding | Update the social image as well as header assets | Publication includes unfurls and caches |

The 1.3× figure compares score gains in an external intonation study. It is not a Lanclo efficacy result. The historical film and current product may describe different question counts or delivery behavior. Keep those contexts separate.
