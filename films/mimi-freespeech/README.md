# Keep talking for 3 minutes — a 2D Mimi short

**English** · [日本語](README.ja.md)

「今までこんな練習、したことあった？」(ever practised like this before?). Lanclo's photo description: look at a photo and speak in your own words, up to 3:00 (the app's own「最大 3:00」). Mimi tries — no script, on her own, the clock fast-forwarded to 3:00 — and gets AI feedback on five points with her words written out. Then her French friend (beret, striped shirt, baguette) tries the app:「3分じゃ短いから、もっと録音伸ばせない？」(three minutes is too short — can't you make the recording longer?). Mimi:「…こっちは3分が長いんだけど」(for me, three minutes is long). Message:「自分で考えて話す力を、伸ばす。」

0:17.7 / 1080×1920, 60 fps / Japanese speech bubbles and captions, Mimi's English and the friend's French by Kokoro TTS / official 2D poses + GSAP, real app (simulator) + HyperFrames

Two cuts: Japanese only (`mimi-freespeech.mp4`) and with English subtitles (`mimi-freespeech-en.mp4`); drafts in Lanclo's Google Drive `002.SNS/021. Keep Talking for 3 Minutes/` (`Free_Speech_draft_2026-10-21.mp4`, `Free_Speech_draft_EN-subs_2026-10-21.mp4`, raw take and clips in `FreeSpeech_assets/`).
