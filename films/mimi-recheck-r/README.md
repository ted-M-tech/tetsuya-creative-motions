# The R Came Back — a 2D Mimi short (draft)

**English** · [日本語](README.ja.md)

Hook from the first frame: 「治ったはずのRが…」 (The R I thought I'd fixed…). A horror parody kept light and cute: a dim living room, and a soft purple ghost shaped like an "L" rises behind the unaware Friend. Flashback 「あの日…」: the Friend nails "That's right!", confetti, Mimi cheers. 「しばらくして…」: the slip comes back, "That's light!", and the L ghost pops up with 「ただいま〜」; Mimi jumps in with her flashlight (「出た〜！」). Then the real app: the personalized daily lesson overview, zoomed on its re-check row 「/ɹ/ (right) · 再確認」, with Mimi's line 「忘れた頃に、また確かめるよ」. Back in the bright room the Friend says it right again and the ghost L waves bye (「またね〜」). End card with the CTA tap.

0:17.6 / 1080×1920, 60 fps / Japanese captions and bubbles, the Friend's English in the owner's own registered voice / official 2D poses + GSAP, real app (simulator, QA fixture with a capture-only re-check row) + HyperFrames
