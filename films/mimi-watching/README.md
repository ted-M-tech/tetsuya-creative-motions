# Mimi is always watching — a 2D Mimi short

**English** · [日本語](README.ja.md)

A playful horror opening: a dark room, only the friend's phone glows, Mimi's eyes shine in the dark and a whisper says「Mimiは、ずっと…あなたの成長を見てるよ…」(Mimi is always… watching you grow…). At 20:00 the practice reminder arrives with the app's real wording for a day with an insight (「Mimiが今日のまとめで気づいたことがあるよ。見てみよう！」), she opens it nervously, and the real Mimi's advice screen shows her habits ("And then", 4 times; the /l/ sound, in 4 recordings), other ways to say it ("After that,") and how a native speaker might say it. 「……なんで知ってるの」— lights on, Mimi:「毎日ちゃんと見てるから、伸びるんだよ！」 Copy:「見られてるから、続く。」 Then「……じゃあ、サボった日も？」— Mimi only smiles, and the lights go out again.

0:18.4 / 1080×1920, 60 fps / Japanese bubbles and captions, no voices / official 2D poses + GSAP, real app (simulator) + HyperFrames

Two cuts: Japanese only (`mimi-watching.mp4`) and with English subtitles (`mimi-watching-en.mp4`); drafts in Lanclo's Google Drive `002.SNS/024. Mimi Is Always Watching/` (`Watching_draft_2026-10-21.mp4`, `Watching_draft_EN-subs_2026-10-21.mp4`, raw take in `Watching_assets/`).
