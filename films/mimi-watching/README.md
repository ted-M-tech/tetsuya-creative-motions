# Mimi is always watching — a 2D Mimi short

**English** · [日本語](README.ja.md)

A playful horror opening: a dark room, only the friend's phone glows, Mimi's eyes shine in the dark and a whisper says「Mimiは、ずっと…あなたの成長を見てるよ…」(Mimi is always… watching you grow…). At 20:00 the practice reminder arrives with the app's real wording for a day with an insight (「Mimiが今日のまとめで気づいたことがあるよ。見てみよう！」), she opens it nervously, and the real Mimi's advice screen shows her habits ("And then", 4 times; the /l/ sound, in 4 recordings), other ways to say it ("After that,") and how a native speaker might say it. 「……なんで知ってるの」— lights on, and Mimi explains: she finds the habits you can't see yourself (always the same phrases and words, "And then" 4回), tells you how a native speaker would say it, and its bookmark saves the phrase to My Notes, your review list. Copy:「気づかない癖まで、Mimiが見つける。」 Then the lights dim again, her eyes glow, and she whispers「だから…僕のこと、ずーーーっと忘れないでね…」. The dark scenes have a quiet code-made horror bed (a detuned music-box lullaby, a heartbeat, a swelling string cluster) that cuts to silence at the light switch, where the cheerful theme comes in.

0:22.0 / 1080×1920, 60 fps / Japanese bubbles and captions, no voices / official 2D poses + GSAP, real app (simulator) + HyperFrames

Two cuts: Japanese only (`mimi-watching.mp4`) and with English subtitles (`mimi-watching-en.mp4`); final files in Lanclo's Google Drive `002.SNS/024. Mimi Is Always Watching/` (`Watching_2026-10-21.mp4`, `Watching_EN-subs_2026-10-21.mp4`, raw take in `Watching_assets/`).
