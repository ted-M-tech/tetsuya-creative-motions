## Intent

Mimi short #4 「日記、3日で終わる」 for Instagram Reels / TikTok. Day 3 of her English diary: Mimi has literally become a little monk (三日坊主) knocking a wooden fish. At the desk the blank page wears her out; she taps Lanclo's English journal and just talks ("Yesterday... I go to cafe."), a diary check card gives one fix (go → went), she hears the corrected diary in her own voice, reads along, and the calendar fills day 4 to 7. 「三日坊主、卒業。」 1 s end card. 2D official illustration style.
