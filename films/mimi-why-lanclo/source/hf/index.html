At a discussion abroad, I was the only one left out.
Everyone else was talking like crazy.
In Japan, I studied English with vocabulary, grammar and silent reading.
I could speak in situations I knew.
But all I did was repeat them.
The people I met abroad spoke with confidence.
Even if reading was hard for them.
The opposite of me, a Japanese learner. (Me: reading ◎ speaking △ / Them: reading △ speaking ◎)
What the real world needs is the power to think for yourself, speak and discuss. (Meetings · Debates · Small talk)
A university teacher who speaks five languages says:
“Think for yourself, speak, and fix it. And keep repeating that.”
Think → Speak → Fix, again and again.
When you try to speak, you notice what you can’t say yet. (The Output Hypothesis in second language acquisition research)
That’s why Lanclo, on purpose, has features that need your voice, and features you can’t do without thinking.
