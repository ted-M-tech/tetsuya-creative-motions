# Why Lanclo: Speak, Think, Improve — a 2D Mimi short

**English** · [日本語](README.ja.md)

The founder's reason for Lanclo, in 73 seconds. Hook from the first frame:「海外の議論の場で、僕だけぽつん。」(at a discussion abroad, I was the only one left out). Bruno, Pippa and Coco talk fast and over each other (Kokoro English chatter) while Mimi (the founder) shrinks, tries to speak and closes her mouth. Flashback to studying in Japan (vocabulary book, grammar drills, silent reading), then the classic canned self-introduction: Bruno, Pippa and Coco ask "Where are you from?", "What do you like?", "Nice to meet you!" and Mimi gives the same three answers every time ("I'm from Japan." "I like traveling." "Nice to meet you!"), the bubbles piling up and the counter climbing ×1 → ×100 —「知ってる会話なら、言える。でも、それをくり返すだけだった。」 One question deeper ("Oh, why do you like traveling?") and Mimi goes blank (「…」). The people Mimi met abroad speak with confidence, grammar mistakes and all ("Yesterday I go to the meeting, and I say my idea!"), (Coco: "I think this plan no good, because too much money!") — 「文法はめちゃくちゃ。でも、堂々と意見を言う。」 — though Pippa squints at a written page — 「日本人の僕とは、逆。」 The real world needs the power to think, speak and discuss (meetings, debates, small talk). The university teacher who speaks five languages and is Lanclo's co-developer and advisor (the Friend in a red beret, as in 025; label 「Lanclo共同開発・監修」):「自分で考えて、話して、直す。それをくり返すこと。」, shown as a Think → Speak → Fix cycle. A calm research card: trying to speak shows you what you can't say yet (Swain's Output Hypothesis, 1985). 「だからLancloは、あえて。」 — features that need your voice and features you can't do without thinking. Then an English-learning skill map (2×2: input ↔ output/voice, fixed answers ↔ thinking for yourself; features designed under the lecturer's supervision): as each real-app feature appears — daily lesson, photo description (up to 3 minutes), English journal, conversation practice, debate — its puzzle pieces fill with Lanclo blue; then ⑥ phrase review (swipe) fills the vocabulary/phrases piece in pale blue: 「インプットも、スワイプでサクッと。でも力を入れているのは、声に出して考えるほう。」 Grammar and reading stay empty (「ここは机でできる」). Mimi closes, warmly:「机に向かう練習のほうが、ラクだよね。わかる。」「でも、自分で考えて話すと、今まで見えなかった苦手が見えてくる。」「大変だけど、僕が自然に支えるしくみを用意したから。」「一緒にがんばろう！」, then 「日本の英語を、声から変える。」 and the end card with the CTA tap.
