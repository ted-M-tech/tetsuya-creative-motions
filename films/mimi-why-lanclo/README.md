# Why Lanclo: Speak, Think, Improve — a 2D Mimi short

**English** · [日本語](README.ja.md)

The founder's reason for Lanclo, in 55 seconds. Hook from the first frame:「海外の議論の場で、僕だけぽつん。」(at a discussion abroad, I was the only one left out). Bruno, Pippa and Coco talk fast and over each other (Kokoro English chatter) while Mimi (the founder) shrinks, tries to speak and closes her mouth. Flashback to studying in Japan (vocabulary book, grammar drills, silent reading), then the classic canned self-introduction: Bruno, Pippa and Coco ask "Where are you from?", "What do you like?", "Nice to meet you!" and Mimi gives the same three answers every time ("I'm from Japan." "I like traveling." "Nice to meet you!"), the bubbles piling up and the counter climbing ×1 → ×100 —「知ってる会話なら、言える。でも、それをくり返すだけだった。」 One question deeper ("Oh, why do you like traveling?") and Mimi goes blank (「…」). The people Mimi met abroad speak with confidence, grammar mistakes and all ("Yesterday I go to the meeting, and I say my idea!"), though Pippa squints at a written page — 「日本人の僕とは、逆。」 The real world needs the power to think, speak and discuss (meetings, debates, small talk). The university teacher who speaks five languages (the Friend in a red beret, as in 025):「自分で考えて、話して、直す。それをくり返すこと。」, shown as a Think → Speak → Fix cycle. A calm research card: trying to speak shows you what you can't say yet (Swain's Output Hypothesis, 1985). 「だからLancloは、あえて。」 — features that need your voice and features you can't do without thinking — and a real-app montage: photo description (up to 3 minutes), debate, conversation practice (roleplay), English journal, daily lesson. Close:「日本の英語を、声から変える。」 End card with the CTA tap.
