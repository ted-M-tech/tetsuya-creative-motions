# Cloning: Shadowing, but in Your Own Voice

**Draft (2026-10-23), awaiting the owner's review.** Lanclo value film #067 for people who tried shadowing and gave up. Frame 1: 「シャドーイング、続かなかった人へ」 (For anyone who gave up on shadowing) while the model runs ahead and the Friend lags behind on two voice lanes (ズレズレ…). The Friend: "I tried it so many times on YouTube and Netflix. But it was hard…" A 3-2-1 countdown of reasons, accent last: 3 声質 (a deep and a high voice), 2 リズム (a fast, linked line, "Whaddaya wanna do aboudit?"), 1 アクセント (US, then UK, AU, …? which one do I copy?) → 「自分に合うお手本、探すのが いちばん難しい」. Mimi's idea: AI can make voices now and many businesses use it — why not pronunciation practice? The word SHADOWING is struck through and rewritten into CLONING: 「名付けて"クローニング"！」「僕が勝手に名付けたんだ。かっこいいでしょ？」. The research card in the LP's wording (English intonation test, about 1.3× the score gain; native model +65.52 vs own-voice model +86.61; 66 learners, 12 weeks, not a test of Lanclo; Li, Lian & Yodkamlue 2020), the Friend: 「すごい！」「たしかに…自分の声なら、まねしやすい！」. The real daily lesson with the model in your own voice, 「これがクローニング！」, 「よし、使ってみよう！」 and the end card.
