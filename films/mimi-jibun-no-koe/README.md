# My own voice? No way — a 2D Mimi short

**English** · [日本語](README.ja.md)

Mimi cringes at her own recorded English, hears her-voice native model, practises along and is convinced by a score and one fix. 0:15, 1080×1920, 60 fps. Official art style extended with Codex, animated with Rive bones, finished in HyperFrames.
