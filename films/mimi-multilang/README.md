# Your voice in every language — a 2D Mimi short

**English** · [日本語](README.ja.md)

Within the first second, Mimi's friend greets in language after language — Hello! Bonjour ! ¡Hola! 你好！ 안녕하세요! Ciao! Olá! नमस्ते! … — every ~0.33 s, all "in her own AI voice". The copy card says「17言語、ぜんぶ自分の声。」(17 languages, all your own voice) over the app's 17 study languages. Mimi: "Register your voice once. Then just switch the language!" The real app (simulator, fixture data): AI Voice → 🌐 language menu → Français → 読み込み中… → the 将来の発音 passage in French, 試聴 plays; then 한국어 shows the Korean passage. The friend: "I'll practise for my Korea trip next month!" — end card.

0:18.7 / 1080×1920, 60 fps / Japanese speech bubbles and captions / official 2D poses + GSAP, real app (simulator) + HyperFrames

**The voices are a TTS stand-in, not a voice clone**: Kokoro, one female voice per language where Kokoro has one (English af_bella, French ff_siwis, Spanish ef_dora, Mandarin zf_xiaobei, Italian if_sara, Brazilian Portuguese pf_dora, Hindi hf_alpha). Korean, German, Russian, Turkish, Vietnamese and Japanese are bubbles only. The film says so on screen (※動画の音声は合成音声によるイメージです).

Two cuts: Japanese only (`mimi-multilang.mp4`) and with English subtitles (`mimi-multilang-en.mp4`); drafts in Lanclo's Google Drive `002.SNS/022. Your Voice in Every Language/` (`Multilang_draft_2026-10-21.mp4`, `Multilang_draft_EN-subs_2026-10-21.mp4`, raw take in `Multilang_assets/`).
