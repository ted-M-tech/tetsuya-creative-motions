# How a Quitter Kept Going for a Month — a 2D Mimi short

**English** · [日本語](README.ja.md)

Hook from the first frame:「3日坊主の私が、1ヶ月続いた理由」(I always quit after three days — here's how I kept going for a month). Mimi's friend appears as a little "three-day monk"; a red "3日" stamp is struck out and a "🔥30日" badge slams in. A quick flashback shows her usual pattern (day 1 ◎, day 2 ◯, day 3 △, day 4 ✕ asleep). Then Lanclo's widgets keep pulling her back: Mimi on the Lock Screen (Journal, Add phrase), the Daily lesson widget on the Home Screen with its streak badge, a tap opens the real daily lesson. On a lazy night the 20:00 practice reminder arrives with the app's real wording for a day with an insight (「Mimiが今日のまとめで気づいたことがあるよ。見てみよう！」), and Mimi's daily report shows her habits. A month later the real Home screen shows a 30-day streak. Copy:「続いたのは、Mimiがいたから。」

0:19.0 / 1080×1920, 60 fps / Japanese captions and bubbles, no voices / official 2D poses + GSAP, real app (simulator, QA fixtures) + HyperFrames

Two cuts: Japanese only (`mimi-quitter.mp4`) and with English subtitles (`mimi-quitter-en.mp4`); final files in Lanclo's Google Drive `002.SNS/029. How a Quitter Kept Going for a Month/` (`Quitter_2026-10-21.mp4`, `Quitter_EN-subs_2026-10-21.mp4`, raw take in `Quitter_assets/`).
