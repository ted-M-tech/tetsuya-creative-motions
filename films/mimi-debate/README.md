# Lost an argument in English — a 2D Mimi short

**English** · [日本語](README.ja.md)

Mimi's friend corners her in English:「Dog person or cat person?」Mimi can't answer and deflates. The hook card says「言い返せないなら、ディベートで練習！」(can't answer back? practise with Debate), and Lanclo's real Debate practice plays in the simulator: pick a topic (お金と幸せ) and a position (賛成), record your opinion (up to 3 minutes a turn), get feedback on the phrasing, the AI pushes back with an opposing view and a question, record your reply. Rematch: the same question, Mimi answers at once with a reason and asks back「How about you?」— now the friend stammers, and comes back with「…てか、あんたうさぎだけどね」(…also, you're a rabbit).

0:20.5 / 1080×1920, 60 fps / Japanese speech bubbles and captions, English lines (Mimi's answer in the owner's own registered Lanclo voice, the friend's question Kokoro) / official 2D poses + GSAP, real app (simulator, QA fixture) + HyperFrames

Two cuts: Japanese only (`mimi-debate.mp4`) and with English subtitles (`mimi-debate-en.mp4`); final files in Lanclo's Google Drive `002.SNS/019. Lost an Argument in English/` (`Debate_2026-10-21.mp4`, `Debate_EN-subs_2026-10-21.mp4`, raw take in `Debate_assets/`).

The "win" is only the comedy scene; the app names no winner and does not judge opinions.
