# One loop in Lanclo

**English** · [日本語](README.ja.md)

Mimi's friend studies English with a generic AI chat: 「"touch base"ってどういう意味…？」, a wall of text floods the screen, and other study apps (AI chat, flashcards, notes, pronunciation videos; no brands) are piled up behind the phone. The history shows the same question three times: 「昨日も同じこと聞いてる…」. Mimi presents 「AI英語学習の悩み TOP7（Mimi調べ）」 (marked 「※Mimiの独断です」), counting down from No. 7 to No. 1 「聞いたら終わりで、すぐ忘れる」 while the friend nods 「わかる…」. Mimi: 「Lancloなら、全部まとめて回るよ」. Copy: 「続けられて、変化が見える。」 Then a loop with Lanclo (the real app, simulator) in the middle and seven stops, 1.7 s each. Mimi runs round it; each stop shows a real screen and ticks off the ranked struggle it solves: ① the model in your own voice (No. 6) ② five-angle scores (No. 4) ③ Mimi corrects what you said (No. 3) ④ Mimi's advice ⑤ one press on the Action Button opens today's lesson (No. 2) ⑥ the study record (No. 5) ⑦ save in one tap, and review brings it back right before you'd forget (No. 7 and No. 1). On a fast lap a score ticks up (「※数値はイメージ」). The friend: 「自然に続けられて、変化が見れる…これが大事なんだよね」. One week later, in bed at night, swiping review cards: 「やば…Lanclo沼から抜けられない…」. Mimi: 「それ、続いてるってことだよ」. End card.

0:30.2 / 1080×1920, 60 fps / two cuts: Japanese only, and with English subtitles / 2D Mimi and her friend, simulator recordings in a phone frame / no voices (speech bubbles)
