# One loop in Lanclo

**English** · [日本語](README.ja.md)

Mimi's friend studies English with a generic AI chat: 「"touch base"ってどういう意味…？」, a wall of text floods the screen, and other study apps (AI chat, flashcards, notes, pronunciation videos; no brands) are piled up behind the phone. The history shows the same question three times: 「昨日も同じこと聞いてる…」. Mimi presents 「AI英語学習の悩み TOP7（Mimi調べ）」 (marked 「※Mimiの独断です」), counting down from No. 7 to No. 1 「聞いたら終わりで、すぐ忘れる」, one second per item, while the friend nods 「わかる…」. Mimi: 「Lancloなら、全部まとめて回るよ」. Copy: 「続けられて、変化が見える。」 The seven struggles fly onto a ring around Lanclo, each landing on the stop that solves it. Mimi runs one lap in learning order, 1.25 s per stop. At each stop the real screen shows, the struggle is struck out, and its card flips to Lanclo's answer: the entrance (Action Button and the daily-lesson widget), your own lesson, the model in your own voice, Mimi's advice, saving with the bookmark, swipe review, the study record. Then 「→ 次のレッスンに反映」 and a fast lap with a score (「※数値はイメージ」). The friend: 「自然に続けられて、変化が見れる…これが大事なんだよね」. One week later, in bed at night, swiping review cards: 「やば…Lanclo沼から抜けられない…」. Mimi: 「それ、続いてるってことだよ」. End card.

0:30.1 / 1080×1920, 60 fps / two cuts: Japanese only, and with English subtitles / 2D Mimi and her friend, simulator recordings in a phone frame / no voices (speech bubbles)
