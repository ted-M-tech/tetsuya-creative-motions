# One loop in Lanclo

**English** · [日本語](README.ja.md)

Mimi's friend has five study apps on her phone (a look-up AI, a notes app for copy-paste, an AI chat app, pronunciation videos, flashcards; all generic, no brands). Each one starts, stops halfway and gathers dust: 「勉強が続かない…」. Mimi: 「それ、全部ひとつで回るよ」. Copy: 「バラバラの勉強を、ひとつのループに。」 Then a loop with Lanclo (the real app, simulator) in the middle and seven stops; Mimi runs round it and each stop shows the real screen for 2–3 s: ① pronunciation practice (the model is your own voice) ② weak points on five angles ③ situation conversation and English journal ④ Mimi's advice (your habits, 「ネイティブなら」) ⑤ the daily lesson built on the habits Mimi noticed ⑥ the learning record (pronunciation balance and trend) ⑦ swipe review. The second and third laps speed up while an illustrative score ticks up (labelled 「※数値はイメージ」). The friend: 「…ずっと回せるやつじゃん」. End card.

0:30.2 / 1080×1920, 60 fps / two cuts: Japanese only, and with English subtitles / 2D Mimi and her friend, a simulator recording in a phone frame / no voices (speech bubbles)
