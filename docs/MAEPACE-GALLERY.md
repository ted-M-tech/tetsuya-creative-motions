## Unit of publication

One completed LP is one work. One completed film is one work. Supporting screenshots, prompts, process decks and skills belong within that artifact's making-of, not as additional portfolio entries. The existing teaching deck remains in the fixed open-works-v1 source edition but is not advertised as a separate completed slide project.

Two completed artifacts are currently listed: Lanclo LP and Lanclo film. Add future LPs, films and slides as complete entries in MaePace's central data and the same gallery component; do not manufacture counts or placeholders to imply a larger catalog.
